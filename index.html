I work where hospitality meets software

I spent more than four years in Bhutan's luxury tourism sector, building guest programmes, managing hotels and reservations, and selling trips to the people who travel here. That job taught me how a guest's day really runs, and where things quietly break.

I've been into tech since high school, where I first tried HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I then did an 18-month IT diploma at Rigsum Institute of IT, where I went deeper into those and learned Java, PHP, C, C++ and WordPress, along with computer hardware and networking basics. After that I took a three-month UI/UX class at a startup, learning Figma, Adobe XD, and how to pitch and market an idea.

I then interned as a business analyst at SELISE, where I learned to write business requirement, functional specification and technical specification documents, plus test cases and test plans. I also learned how Waterfall and Agile work, including Scrum and sprints, and sat in on Scrum meetings. Now I'm building my own projects and learning how to make websites and tools that people can actually use. My family roots are in Bumthang, my village is in Trongsa, and I'm based in Thimphu.
